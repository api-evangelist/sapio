Key points At SapioCon 2026, Amy Yeung, head of cellular services within scientific operations at the Wellcome Sanger Institute, made a practical case for why scientific ambition now depends on operational foundations. At Sanger’s scale, operations are not a support function in the background. Instead, they are, as Yeung described them, the enablers of science.
